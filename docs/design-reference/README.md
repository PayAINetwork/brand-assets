## Which rules apply?

The [repository README](../../README.md), [UI design prompt](../../prompts/ui-design-style.md), and [license](../../LICENSE) remain the published baseline. Product UI keeps the existing palette and component styling. Marketing and social work may use the observed profiles when deliberately selected for the brief. The guides do not silently replace product defaults.

The primary blue remains **#4D63F6**. Stronger blue **#1D45D8**, gradients, and the sampled 8px marketing CTA radius are scoped marketing observations. Published product buttons retain their 12px radius and flat blue surface. Always provide visible keyboard focus, including when adapting the older prompt's outline styling.

Both facilitator and merchant-layer messaging routes are included as communication patterns. They do not establish live capability, performance, partner endorsement, or product availability.

## Publication scope

This edition contains no original draft screenshots, raw design-file links/IDs, sample personal details, unrelated concepts, or draft business metrics. Original source-review materials are maintained separately. Asset usage remains subject to the repository license.

For a new design, read both guides, choose the appropriate context, use the repository assets, verify claims, and record proposed deviations.
